**New public profile. Working projects.** I make tools for people who want the point without spending two hours digging for it. They show what was said, where it came from, and what deserves a closer look. If a motive is only a guess, it stays a guess.

## Start here

### [Leave YouTube](https://github.com/eochu-ollathair/leave-youtube)

Pick subjects and creators. Set how many recent videos to read. Block the ones you are tired of. Get the useful speech in a short morning Telegram report instead of sitting through the adverts.

### [Leave the Podcasts](https://github.com/eochu-ollathair/leave-podcasts)

Follow the shows you choose and add a few episodes popular in Ireland. Read the points from each show separately, with your own sceptical view underneath when you connect your own text AI.

## Make them yours

Both projects are free to download. Give either project page to an AI assistant and ask it to set up your own copy and send a real test report. You need a Mac or Linux computer and your own Telegram bot; the basic reports need no paid AI account.
