I build things that help people find the interesting bit: a place they did not know was nearby, or the words in a call that a score missed.

## Discover Alone and Dagmor

### [Discover Alone](https://discoveralone.com/)

A free map of more than a million places, people and events across Ireland, England, Scotland and Wales. Search a town or use your location. Explorer Tom gets excited about the odd places, tells playful stories and gives tips for the trip. [Start with four places in Carndonagh](https://discoveralone.com/assets/guide/carndonagh-field-sheet.html).

### [Dagmor](https://dagmor.app/)

Send completed customer calls and get clear reports, or use the full call review system. Dagmor puts transcripts beside recordings, checks a chosen script or drafts one from repeated points in calls, and gives people the final say on disputed findings. Its reports cover agent Hero Scores, conduct, recording gaps, trends and customers' own words between survey answers. [Try the free call-sampling calculator](https://dagmor.app/story-call-sampling-calculator.html).

## Other public projects

### [Leave YouTube](https://github.com/eochu-ollathair/leave-youtube)

Pick subjects and creators. Set how many recent videos to read. Block the ones you are tired of. Get the useful speech in a short morning Telegram report instead of sitting through the adverts.

### [Leave the Podcasts](https://github.com/eochu-ollathair/leave-podcasts)

Follow the shows you choose and add a few episodes popular in Ireland. Read the points from each show separately, with your own sceptical view underneath when you connect your own text AI.

## Make them yours

Both projects are free to download. Give either project page to an AI assistant and ask it to set up your own copy and send a real test report. You need a Mac or Linux computer and your own Telegram bot; the basic reports need no paid AI account.
